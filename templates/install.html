  1. Open the downloaded entropicide.apk (from the notification, or Files → Downloads).
  2. If Android says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then open Entropicide and sign in.
  4. For reminders: Settings → Notifications → Turn on for this device, and allow notifications when Android asks.

Entropicide then also appears in other apps' Share menu: share a page, photo or file to it and it lands in your Inbox.

The app opens this site in Chrome's engine, full screen, so Entropicide itself updates by itself. When the app around it changes, Entropicide offers the new version when you open it: tap Update, then open the download and tap Update again. Chrome must be installed on the phone.

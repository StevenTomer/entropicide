What it stores

Your account name, a hashed password, and the tasks, projects, areas, tags and links you enter. Everything is kept in a database on this server and is visible only to you.

Google Calendar and Contacts

If you connect Google Calendar, Entropicide uses Google's permission to:

  • see your email address, to show which account is connected;
  • list your calendars and read their upcoming events, to show them alongside your tasks;
  • add, change and remove events for your dated tasks on the one calendar you choose, and read changes you make to those events;
  • if you allow it, read your contacts' names, nicknames, phone numbers, email addresses and postal addresses (never changing them), so you can name people in your tasks and call, text, email or find them from there. A copy is kept on this server and refreshed every hour.

Calendar and contact data is used only to show it to you and keep your tasks in step. It is not shared with anyone, sold, or used for advertising or any other purpose. Entropicide's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting in Settings removes the events Entropicide added, revokes its access, and deletes the stored Google tokens, cached events and the copy of your contacts. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
